Scoping note

A from-scratch build of this scope was ballparked at $15,000–$40,000 (roughly ₦20M–₦60M at current rates). Weighted below by module rather than split evenly — Finance & Accounts (300) carries the largest share even though it lists fewer areas than HR (100), because its build risk sits in ledger correctness: a double-entry system that must always balance, AP/AR with partial payments and credit notes, bank reconciliation, and financial-statement generation, not just screen count.
